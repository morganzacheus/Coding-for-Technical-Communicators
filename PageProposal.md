## Purpose, Audience, and Alignment 
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; This semester I am taking this course and Foundations of Technical Communication with Dr. Dayley. In Dr. Dayley's course one of our two projects is to create 
a portfolio website. The combination of these two courses seem perfect to give me the time and guidance to truly develop an online portfolio, a feat that I have never considered and 
would be intimidated to attempt alone. The purpose of my project will be to create a professional portfolio. In Dr. Dayley's course, part of our weekly assignments has been analyzing 
established professionals' portfolios to brainstorm ideas for our own development goals. For me, I have three primary aspects of how I am as a professional that I want to communicate 
through my e-portfolio: 
1.  Focusing on my academic identity as a **technical communicator**. This identity, highlighting education and completed projects, is meant for my intended audience of traditional
and non-traditional employers. Non-traditional employers, for me, would be creative writers, second-language learners, and those interested in professional tutoring/editing for
their writing project(s).
2. Building off of the previous sentence, I want to emphasis the experience that makes me well-rounded and competent to pursue **freelance work or a small business**. I spent 5 years
as a professional writing center tutor at two separate universities in North Carolina and Texas. I have a Bachelor's in Spanish and can speak and write the language fluently. And,
before I was accepted into this program, I was also accepted in the English department's Creative Writing program where I studied and practiced craft, form, and theories for a year.
3. A **modern but simple** design is an essential part of this project to me. Before a potential employer/customer reads or reviews anything in the e-portfolio, the design will
act a first impression for many. Design deliverables will be a cohesive color scheme and formatting, clear site sections, and some kind of slightly-snazzy technical component possibly
on the homepage or portfolio? Just to show I tried (and succeeded! (hopefully...)).
